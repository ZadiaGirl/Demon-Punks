---
title: Aid & Rebuild
---

The party meats [[Demii]], a lone demon hunter, who introduces the party to [[Jah]], a technician who is able to install and upgrade mods. Later they take the job to _Aid & Rebuild_ the mines in Brimsdale after a demon attack. Solidifying the alliance between Demii and the rest of the party. Finding the miners trapped inside have been infected with demonphage. They discover the water in the mines washes the infection from the miners, allowing them to save their lives.
