Corruption is the level of mental and physical decay caused by the use of [[Mods#Complex|complex mods]]. Each day you lose 1 level of corruption. 
# Level 1
- -1 to ability checks and attack rolls

# Level 2
- -1 to saving throws, disadvantage on efficiency saves

# Level 3
- -10 to your max HP, -2 to AC

# Level 4
- disadvantage on all ability checks

# Level 5
- disadvantage on saving throws

# Level 6
- -10 to max HP, all attacks that hit you become auto crits

# Level 7
- Lose your mind. You become aggressive to your party members.
