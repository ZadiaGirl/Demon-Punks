# Bleeding
On the start of the of the creatures turn, they take 1d6 damage. You can use your action to stop the bleeding, or you can remake the saving throw at the end of their turn to stop the bleeding. 

# Poisoned
A creature that is poisoned takes 1d4 poison damage at the start of their turn. While poisoned they make Con saves at disadvantage. They can remake the save at the end of their turn.
