# Level 1
Use 1 unit of demon mater in order to gain one of these buffs. You can only have 1 buff active at a given time. Each buff lasts for 1 day.
- Health Buff:
	Gain 10 temp HP
- Damage Buff:
	Increase your damage by 1d4
- Senses Buff:
	Increase your perception and investigation checks by 1d4
- Speed Buff:
	Increase your speed by 10'
- Attack Buff:
	Increase your attack rolls by 1d4
- Defense Buff:
	Increase your AC by 1

# Crafting Bench:
Use demon parts to create _simple_ weapons, mods, and armor.
